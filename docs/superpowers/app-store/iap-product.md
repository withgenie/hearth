# In-App Purchase status — not used in Hearth 1.1.0

Hearth 1.1.0 is a paid-upfront Mac App Store app. It has no In-App Purchase,
subscription, evaluation gate, restore button, or StoreKit product metadata.

The previous Hearth Pro IAP concept was not shipped and must not be entered in
App Store Connect or mentioned in product copy. Any future change to a free
download plus Pro unlock requires a separate product decision, implementation,
migration for existing paid customers, StoreKit review, and updated privacy and
review notes.
